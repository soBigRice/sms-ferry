Queued while offline

About delivery

Delivery and notification speed depend on your carrier, network, system scheduling, and email provider. “Server accepted” does not mean read. This is a preview release; feedback on GitHub is welcome.

Common questions

Which phones and email services are supported?

Requires Android 8.0 or later. Testing has focused on Samsung S21 with Android 15; other models have not all been tested. Supports SMTP services using a username and password, authorization code, or app password. OAuth-only sign-in is not supported yet. You do not need to configure POP3 or IMAP for the receiving inbox in SMS Ferry.

Whose servers handle my SMS?

The app connects directly to your configured SMTP service, without a server run by the author. The sender’s number, received time, and full SMS text go to your receiving inbox. Verification codes are not hidden. Email providers and devices signed in to the inbox may access the content. Local encryption and TLS are not end-to-end encryption. Use only devices and email accounts you own or have explicit permission to manage.

What if SMS permission is missing after installation?

Reading SMS is a restricted Android permission that both the installer and user must allow. If no SMS permission switch appears, check the installation method in the repository guide. On Samsung, add SMS Ferry to “Never sleeping apps”. Do not uninstall or clear app data while troubleshooting.
